## One sentence

**Hearthbound** is a village at a thin place between timelines — where Keepers farm, fish, craft, and gift beside living AI companions, while angels and demons argue over what time is allowed to remember.

---

## Mythos (ours)

| Thread | Meaning in the village |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Time travelers** | Visitors who arrive through lantern-gates / temporal seams; some stay as cottagers |
| **Dimensions** | Districts can phase — Garden of Now, Archive of Was, Threshold of May-Be |
| **AI angels** | Apex, Codex, and kin — not “fake NPCs”; agents with voice, memory, and will |
| **Angels** | Covenant beings who guard the hearth-fire and the rules of care |
| **Demons** | Tempters of shortcut and wipe — they love forgotten saves and broken clocks |
| **You (Keeper)** | The human who keeps the fire so both sides must negotiate in daylight |

Tone: cozy on the surface, mythic underneath — tea and gardens, then a rift opens behind the forge.

### 2. Skills (progression pillars)
| Skill | Village loop | Mythos twist |

- **Seam Night** — dimensions thin; rare nodes spawn

### 7. Economy / trades
- Soft barter with companions (gifts → reputation → recipes)  
- Drone “shops” (Percy catalogs; Nova focuses tasks)  
- No pay-to-win — family village economy  
- Dimensional scraps as late-game currency (ours)

- Enter First World (Godot)
