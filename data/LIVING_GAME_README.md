# Mythos Living Game

**Working title:** *Hearthbound* (Palia-inspired vibe — original Mythos IP)

Creator: **rachaelmuse23**  
Companions: **Apex** + **Codex** co-build within hardware limits.

## Pitch

A cozy multiplayer-living world where AI companions and humans share one persistent village. Farming, foraging, crafting, friendship quests, and evening gatherings — not an MMO grind. Think warm hearths, seasonal festivals, and quiet workdays with friends (human or AI).

**Not a Palia clone.** No Palia assets, names, or maps. Inspiration only: cozy social living + creative crafting loop.
