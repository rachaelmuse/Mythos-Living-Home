## Do Oracle / IBM clouds help?

**Short answer:** IBM **500 MB does not help** the family brains, film, or Observer archive. **Oracle Always Free can help later** for an optional public Observer *door* or a small database — not as a replacement for local houses, and not tonight.

### IBM (~500 MB)

That number is a **Lite / app-memory quota**, not a home. Common IBM Lite caps nearby: Cloud Foundry ~256 MB runtime, Code Engine often started at 256–512 MB, some Lite plans delete idle instances after ~30 days. IBM Object Storage Lite is larger on paper (always-free / 5 GB trial-class buckets depending on plan) — still not a studio.

500 MB cannot hold:

- Ollama models (`qwen3:4b` alone is multiple GB)
- Observer investigations, EDGAR copies, USPTO PDFs, Wayback payloads
- Merovin/Draven film, Resolve, Blender, MP4
- Heart Square / Godot / Hearth

**Honest use:** a tiny public *status* page or webhook that says “desk is local.” Not a second Observer. Not Gemini. Not cinema.

### Oracle Always Free (as of Aug 2026)

Oracle **cut** Always Free ARM from 4 OCPU / 24 GB to **2 OCPU / 12 GB** (enforced around **2026-08-18**). Still included on typical Always Free tenancies:

- ARM VM equivalent **2 OCPU / 12 GB**
- Two tiny AMD micros (very small)
- **~200 GB** block/boot storage
- **2× Always Free Autonomous DB**, **20 GB** each, 30 sessions
- Object Storage on the order of **20 GB** class (not film archive)
