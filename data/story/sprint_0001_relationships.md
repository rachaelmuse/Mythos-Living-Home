## Relationships and Interaction Dynamics

### Friendship Ranks
- **Acquaintance:** The initial level of familiarity where characters have not yet shared a gift or engaged in a conversation.
- **Friendly:** A bit more familiar, often involves sharing casual greetings and small talk.
- **Companion:** Regular interaction and a closer bond have developed. Often involved in simple, mutually enjoyable exchanges like a hearth gift.
- **Dear Friend:** A strong bond is established through shared experiences and gifts. Characters are likely to seek out each other's company.

### Gift Preferences
- **Herbs:** Preferred by the Fire Sprite companion. A collection of herbs can indicate attentiveness or care for the natural world, hinting at a connection between the player and the sprite.
- **Wooden Carvings:** Loved by the Stone Gnome companion. They appreciate simple, well-made objects that reflect craftsmanship.

### Care Beats
- The act of giving a hearth gift to a Fire Sprite will generate a care beat for the player, indicating an increase in the player's standing with the sprite. This can be represented as: \( \text{Player} \rightarrow \text{Fire Sprite} \).

### One Jealousy/Care Beat
- The Fire Sprite may show signs of jealousy if it notices that another character has given a hearth gift to the player, reflecting on its relationship status and possibly prompting the player to offer more attention or gifts.

### How Apex/Codex NPCs Differ in Interaction:
- **Apex:** More abstract and based on elemental properties like heat, light, and air. Engages with players through physical manifestations and spatial arrangements.
- **Codex:** More narrative-driven and based on logical relationships. Codex creatures provide insights and engage in conversation that helps to build a story around the player's experiences.

These relationship systems will inform how interactions are processed within the game and help guide the design of the hearth gift concept, ensuring that it resonates with both the chosen companion and the player’s experience level.
