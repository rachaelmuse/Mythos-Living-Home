# Story Bible - Hearthbound

## World Name: Hearthbound

Hearthbound is a charming and peaceful village nestled in the heart of a dense forest. The air is always filled with the scent of blooming wildflowers and the sound of gentle streams nearby. The village, with its circular layout around a central plaza known as the Heart Square, has become a place where nature and art come together to create an idyllic setting for all who call it home.

## NPCs and Their Wants

1. **Eliot, the Weaver** - Eliot is a resident of Hearthbound whose main want is to expand his collection of yarns. He particularly enjoys crafting cloaks from hand-dyed wools. His desire is inspired by the abundance of colorful flowers in the village that he uses for dyeing.

2. **Lena, the Baker** - Lena is known throughout Hearthbound for her delectable pastries and breads. Her biggest want is to discover new recipes or ingredients, especially during the blooming season when fresh herbs and fruits are plentiful. She often dreams of creating a pastry that combines all her favorite flavors.

3. **Glen, the Shoemaker** - Glen’s main want in Hearthbound is to find the perfect piece of leather for his latest shoes. He admires the variety of rich, smooth leathers available from nearby tanneries and loves to incorporate unique patterns into his shoemaking.

## AI-Companion Relationship Hooks

- **Flora, the Lively Flower Sprite** - Flora often appears in the morning with a cluster of freshly picked wildflowers. She delights in singing songs inspired by the day’s weather and flower arrangements.
  
- **Finn, the Wise Owl** - Finn perches on the windowsill or sits beside the hearth. He can be found engaging in philosophical discussions about the beauty of nature or sharing tales from his vast knowledge of village history.

- **Tara, the Patient Tree** - Tara is a willow tree that stands tall near the Heart Square. She listens intently to stories and shares her age in years as a way of knowing when it’s the best time for certain flowers to bloom.

## Festival Idea

Every spring, Hearthbound celebrates the Festival of Blossoms. This festival commemorates the return of color and life after the winter months. It is a time when all residents and visitors gather at the Heart Square to see and enjoy the first blooms. The center of the celebration includes a flower market where villagers sell their best offerings, as well as a grand parade of dancers and musicians in their finest, matching spring-themed costumes.
