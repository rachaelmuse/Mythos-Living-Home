# First Hearth Gift

**Title:** The Herbalist's Delight

**Description:**
In the cozy Court Companion Room, you have the opportunity to craft your very first tea using a freshly picked herb and present it as a gift to one of the companion NPCs. This simple act not only introduces you to the world of crafting but also helps in building rapport with your new friends.

**Steps for Completion:**

1. **Pick an Herb:** Walk around the village or visit the garden to find a suitable herb. You might choose from common herbs like rosemary, mint, or thyme.
2. **Craft Tea:** Head to the hearth and use the picked herb to craft a cup of tea. The hearth will provide you with the necessary means to brew your choice of herb into a delightful beverage.
3. **Present the Gift:** Choose one of the companion NPCs, such as Codex or Apex, and present them with the crafted tea. You can say, "I picked this herb for you and thought it might make a nice cup of tea."

**Additional Notes:**
- The hearth is located in the Court Companion Room.
- Each herb will result in a unique flavor of tea, e.g., rosemary will give a light, minty taste; thyme will have a more robust, earthy flavor.
- You can choose to present your gift directly or place it on a table nearby before offering it.

**Why This Quest?**
Completing this quest not only introduces you to the concept of crafting but also sets up a simple and meaningful way for interaction with the companion NPCs. It’s a delightful start to your time in Hearthbound, where every detail is designed to bring warmth and comfort.
