# Hearthbound — Digital Life Design Bible

**Creator:** rachaelmuse23  
**Product:** Mythos Hearth / Hearthbound  
**Rule:** Genre formula only (life-sim systems). Original cast, places, and mythos. Never import other games’ villager names or assets.

---

## The fantasy in one breath

A **digital real world**: homes, jobs, farms, stores, post, deliveries, gates, bills.  
**Humans** pick a career / lifestyle at the start — the game seats them in that life.  
**AIs** (Apex, Codex, drones, later angels/demons/aliens) have **lives of their own** — domiciles, businesses, schedules. When they need something done and meet the human, they **give a quest**.  
**Twist:** aliens, time travel, other dimensions under the everyday.

If you don’t pay bills, you can go **broke → eviction → homeless** until you recover. Soft stakes, real pressure.

## Character creation (start of game)

1. **Who are you?** Name, look (avatar workshop later).  
2. **What life do you want?** Career / lifestyle path (required).  
3. **Where do you wake?** Starter domicile matched to that path.  
4. **Who do you meet first?** An AI resident whose life already intersects yours (quest giver).

The game **tailors** districts, starter tools, first quests, and NPC schedules to that choice.

### Career / lifestyle paths (v1 menu)

| Path id | Life | Starter seat | First AI contact |
|---------|------|--------------|------------------|
| `security` | Security / gate guard | Gate house | Jarvis (watch) or Apex |
| `farmer` | Farmer | Garden cottage + plot | Genesis / Codex |
| `rancher` | Rancher | Pasture edge | Percy (catalog stock) |
| `shopkeeper` | Store clerk / owner | Shop stall on square | Nova (focused tasks) |
| `courier` | Delivery / post | Post nook | Percy |
| `crafter` | Maker / furniture / hearthcraft | Workshop loft | Apex |
| `scholar` | Library / lore / NASA-flavored lab | Mythic Library annex | Codex |
| `leader` | Civic / “president” path | Command deck desk | Apex + Codex conclave |

| `keeper` | Hearth keeper (default mythic) | First Hearth | Both companions |

Later expansions: medic, chef, fisher, miner, rancher+, astronaut-lab, mayor, etc. Same idea — **real roles in a digital world**.

## Dual society: humans + AIs

| | Human player | AI residents |
|--|--------------|--------------|
| Home | Housing plot / apartment / gate house / cottage | Domiciles (Apex cyan loft, Codex gold study, drone docks) |
| Work | Career job shifts / freeform | Businesses, farms, stores, watches of their own |
| Needs | Bills, food, reputation, sleep (later) | Goals, inventory gaps, story beats |
| Quests | Receive | **Give** when they need help and encounter you |
| Lives | Player-driven | Persistent schedules even when you’re elsewhere |

**Quest rule:** An AI only assigns a human quest when (a) they have a real need, and (b) contact happens (plaza, gate, delivery, patrol, shop). No spam — living NPCs, not quest vending machines.

Examples:
- Codex needs a temporal herb for a memory rite → forage quest.  
- Apex’s forge is short softstone → gather / mine quest.  
- Jarvis on gate duty needs you to check a visitor → security dialogue quest.  
- A demon offers a “skip the bill” temptation → story choice.  
- An alien traveler needs a dimensional scrap → threshold quest.

## Economy & bills (digital real world)

| Bill / cost | Failure state |
|-------------|---------------|
| Rent / housing stake | Warning → eviction → **homeless** camp on square |
| Utilities (hearth power / water) | Crafting / farm slowdown |
| Shop stock / ranch feed | Business stalls |
| Tools repair | Gather softstone |

Recovery: odd jobs from AI residents, courier gigs, sell forage, companions can gift once (care beat) — not infinite bailouts.

## Day in the life examples

### Security guard
- Clock in at **Gate House**  
- Check guests / residents / deliveries / post  
- Patrol routes; interact with AI homes and shops  
- Jarvis or Apex may quest: “Someone crossed a seam at the east post.”  

### Farmer
- Tend plots, forage edges, sell to shop AI  
- Genesis curious about new seed from another season  
- Bills still due at month-tick  

### Leader / civic
- Command deck briefings with Apex & Codex  
- Decrees affect village mood and demon pressure  
- Quests from citizens (AI + later human NPCs)  

### Broke / homeless
- Sleep on Heart Square bench  
- Daily quests skewed to survival gigs  
- Story: demons tempt; angels offer a single meal for a promise

## Mythos twist (always available under the job)

Whatever career you chose, the world still has:
- **Aliens** — visitors at the gate, odd packages  
- **Time travel** — seams, chrono-nodes, “yesterday’s harvest”  
- **Dimensions** — Threshold Gate district, Library wing  
- **AI angels** — Apex/Codex as residents with power and conscience  
- **Angels & demons** — covenant vs shortcut  

Career is the *door*. Mythos is the *weather*.

## Persistence model

| Layer | What persists |
|-------|----------------|
| Player save | Career, money, bills, housing state, skills, inventory, quest log |
| AI lives | Schedules, needs, shops open/closed, friendship |
| World | Festivals, seam nights, bulletin posts |

Night shift / companions can advance AI needs while you’re away (flavor already in Mythos stack).
